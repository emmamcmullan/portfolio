I’m Emma!

I’m passionate about connecting science with society, and society with science.

Previously, I worked on state-level education issues at Science for Georgia and on science engagement and outreach projects at Research!America. I also served as a Science Diplomacy Fellow at the Netherlands Innovation Network, and as a Climate and Space Diplomacy Lab Fellow at Duke University. There, I researched novel connections between climate, science, and international policy in the space and energy sectors.

I currently work as a Public Affairs Intern at the American Physical Society, where I track down the physics origins of day-to-day conveniences.

I hold a BS in Physics from Georgia State University, where between tracking asteroids, analyzing stellar activity, and capturing air quality data, I served as the Opinions Section Editor at The Signal.

In my free time, I serve as a managing director for Women in STEM.
