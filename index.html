About

I’m Emma! I’m passionate about connecting science with society, and society with science. Previously, I worked on state-level education issues at Science for Georgia and on science engagement and outreach projects at Research!America. I also served as a National Science Policy Network Science Diplomacy Fellow at the Netherlands Innovation Network, and as a Climate and Space Diplomacy Lab Fellow at Duke University. I currently work as a Public Affairs Intern at the American Physical Society, where I track down the physics origins of day-to-day conveniences, and create interactive visualizations to show those connections! I hold a BS in Physics from Georgia State University, where between tracking asteroids, analyzing stellar activity, and capturing air quality data, I served as the Opinions Section Editor at The Signal. In my free time, I serve as a managing director for Women in STEM.
