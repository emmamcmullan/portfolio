Broad-band Monitoring of 797 Montana

Bentz, Misty C.; Alahakone, Rachel; Azoulay, Lexi; Burns-Kaurin, Emily; Chaturvedi, Akshat S.; Davis, Madeline; Kimani-Stewart, Karina; Markham, Madison; McGuire, John P.; McMullan, Emma; Patel, Mahir; Binu Raj, Meenakshi; Sankey, Daijha; Wilson, Abeni Amber-Lee; Zdanky, Karisa

The Minor Planet Bulletin, Vol. 52, No. 3, pp. 207–208 (July 2025)

ADS

Abstract. Broad-band monitoring of asteroid 797 Montana was conducted over the course of four nights in 2025 February. Observations from the first three nights were obtained through a Johnson V filter, while the fourth night was collected as unfiltered observations. We find a best-fit rotation period of 4.545 ± 0.001 h and an amplitude of variability of dV = 0.50 ± 0.03 mag.
