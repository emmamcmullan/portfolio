Science

I am interested in both astronomincal and environmental sciences, with a particular interest in optical monitoring methods (especially those that are low-cost). I have logged 36 hours of observing experience at Hard Labor Creek Observatory in Rutelegde, GA, utilizing the Miller Telescope, a 24-inch Planewave f/6.5 Corrected Dall-Kirkham Astrograph. I have additional deploying and collecting data with aethalometers (MA200, UV-IR Black Carbon), laser particle counters (Atmotubes, PurpleAir Flex), and VOC absorbing cartridges (Radiello). I also have experience with HPLCs, spectrophotometers, adruino, and optical stages.

Broad-band Monitoring of 797 Montana

Bentz, Misty C.; Alahakone, Rachel; Azoulay, Lexi; Burns-Kaurin, Emily; Chaturvedi, Akshat S.; Davis, Madeline; Kimani-Stewart, Karina; Markham, Madison; McGuire, John P.; McMullan, Emma; Patel, Mahir; Binu Raj, Meenakshi; Sankey, Daijha; Wilson, Abeni Amber-Lee; Zdanky, Karisa

The Minor Planet Bulletin, Vol. 52, No. 3, pp. 207–208 (July 2025)

View Paper

Abstract

Broad-band monitoring of asteroid 797 Montana was conducted over four nights in 2025 February. Observations from the first three nights were obtained through a Johnson V filter, while the fourth night was collected as unfiltered observations. We find a best-fit rotation period of 4.545 ± 0.001 h and an amplitude of variability of dV = 0.50 ± 0.03 mag.
